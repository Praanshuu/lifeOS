# 🛡️ Priority Manager: The Definitive Behavioral Manual

Priority Manager is a high-performance **Behavioral Operating System** designed to bridge the gap between high-level ambition and daily execution. It is built on the principle of **Manual Intentionality supported by Agentic AI.**

---

## 📖 1. Core Terminologies

| Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective (Goal)** | Vision | High-level ambition (e.g., "Launch Startup"). Restricted by importance slots. |
| **Workstream** | Strategy | A "Composite Task" that acts as a parent to many micro-tasks. |
| **Task** | Tactics | An atomic unit of work. Can be **One-off** or **Recurring** (Habits). |
| **Micro-Task** | Execution | AI-generated daily steps that break down a Workstream. |
| **The Stack** | Daily Plan | Today's committed agenda, split into tiers (Minimum, Target, Stretch, Refresh). |
| **Session** | Tracking | A real-time recording of focus, breaks, or distractions. |

---

## 🏛️ 2. Vision Control (The Database Page)

This is your strategic command center for long-term planning.

### **The Objectives View**
- **Importance Slot Enforcement**: To prevent "priority dilution," the system limits slots for critical work.
    - **Level 5 Goals**: Only 2 slots available.
    - **Level 4 Goals**: 4 slots available.
- **Logical vs. Emotional Drivers**: Every objective requires both a cold logical reason and a warm emotional "Why." The AI uses these to coach you during low-energy periods.
- **Recursive Progress Rollup**: A multi-level bar system that calculates progress from micro-tasks → workstreams → overall goals.

### **Multi-View Task Management**
- **Table View**: High-density view with a deep **Filter Engine** (Priority, Status, Energy Level, Objective).
- **Kanban Board**: A visual pipeline for tracking task movement.
- **Task Inspector**: A detailed side-panel for every task to manage sub-tasks, estimates, and "Anticipated Friction."

---

## ⚡ 3. The Execution Cockpit (Home Page)

Your daily landing page for zero-friction execution.

### **Phase A: Building the Stack**
- **Manual Backlog**: Pull tasks manually from your backlog.
- **AI Day Planner**: Use **"Generate Plan"** to let the AI build your stack based on deadlines and past performance. Use **"AI Steering"** (e.g., *"Focus on coding today"*) to influence the results.
- **The Commitment Lock**: Clicking **"Commit to Stack"** turns your list into a formal promise. Breaking this locks in a lower **Commitment Score**.

### **Phase B: Real-Time Tracking**
- **The Timer Widget**: The persistent heart of the sidebar. It pulses when you are recording focus.
- **System Activities**: Use the **Take Break** and **Distraction** buttons to log non-work events.
- **Manual Completion**: For work done away from the screen, use the **Manual Completion Modal** in the Task Inspector to retroactively log time and "Friction Notes."

### **Phase C: Daily Reality**
- **Reality Timeline**: A visual graph comparing your **Intended Plan** vs. your **Actual Tracked Behavior**. It exposes exactly where and why your day deviated from your plan.

## 🤖 4. AI & Agentic Capabilities

Priority Manager features a suite of specialized AI engines:

### **1. AI Task Breakdown (Micro-task Engine)**
Found in the **Task Inspector**. Provide guidance like *"5 questions per day"* or *"1 chapter every 2 days,"* and the AI will generate a sequence of daily micro-tasks across your calendar, automatically avoiding your deadline.

### **2. Agentic AI Chat**
A full-featured agent that can modify your database directly via conversation:
- *"Add a high-priority task for tomorrow called 'Client Meeting'."*
- *"Move all my pending tasks for the 'Website' workstream to Friday."*
- *"Delete all tasks related to the old 'Research' project."*

### **3. AI Bodyguard (Radical Truth)**
- **Weekly Behavioral Reports**: AI analyzes the last 14 days of your life to find patterns (e.g., *"You are 40% more likely to get distracted on Tuesdays"*).
- **Reflection Engine**: If you skip a task, you must provide a reason. The Bodyguard analyzes these (Avoidance, Burnout, Distraction, Energy) to coach your discipline.
- **Real-Time Nudges**: Feedback based on your current distraction logs.

## 📜 5. The Ledger (Logs Page)

A transparent history of every minute tracked:
- **Full Session Table**: View, search, and edit every focus, break, and distraction session.
- **Friction Logging**: Review notes on which tasks caused the most mental resistance.
- **Data Integrity**: Manually adjust session times to ensure your AI insights are based on 100% accurate data.

---

## 🚦 6. Pro Workflow Summary

1.  **Vision**: Define 2-3 Level 5 Goals in the **Objectives** view.
2.  **Strategy**: Break down scary goals into **Workstreams**. Use **AI Breakdown** to turn them into daily micro-tasks.
3.  **Planning**: Every morning, pick your **Stack**, set an intention, and **Commit**.
4.  **Execution**: Use the **Timer**. Log **Distractions** honestly.
5.  **Growth**: Check the **AI Bodyguard** weekly. Read the "Radical Truth" and adjust your environment to win.
